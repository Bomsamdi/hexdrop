GRAVITY SHAPES

A block puzzle with gravity, on five different grids. Drop pieces, complete lines, chain cascades — on hexagons, squares, triangles, octagons or a wall of cubes.

Classic

Endless. Fill lines, trigger cascades, beat your best.

Time Attack

90 seconds; every line buys you time.

Daily Challenge

One board a day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak.

Expedition

An endless trail of levels with new board shapes, stones and gems.

Tilt

Gravity turns every few pieces and the whole board slides.

Puzzle Lab

No luck: place the given pieces so the board ends empty.

Duel

Live 1v1 on the same board. Casual or ranked leagues.

Five grids

The same rules on five different geometries. What changes is how many ways a line can run and how a piece lands.

Hex

The board the game started on. Lines run three ways.

Quad

Squares, seven by seven. A line is a whole row or column.

Triangle

Triangles left and right. A piece falls two at a time, so you choose which half it lands on.

Octa

Octagons with a small square in every gap. Every line runs through both.

Cube

A wall of cubes. A line crosses whole cubes — the hardest board here.
